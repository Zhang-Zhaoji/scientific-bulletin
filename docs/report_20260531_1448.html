中文标题: 小鼠初级嗅觉系统中社会气味空间组织与检测

单位: Department Of Physics, Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Harvard University, Cambridge, Ma 02138, Usa; Department Of Chemistry And Chemical Biology, Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Harvard University, Cambridge, Ma 02138, Usa; Department Of Molecular And Cellular Biology, Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Harvard University, Cambridge, Ma 02138, Usa 等

核心优势: 结合Zhuang实验室顶尖的MERFISH空间转录组技术与Dulac实验室深厚的行为神经生物学背景，实现了从单细胞分辨率到系统级映射的跨越，提供了前所未有的高分辨率嗅觉地图。
